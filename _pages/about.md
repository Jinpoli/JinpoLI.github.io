I am a PhD candidate in Economics at [Lingnan University](https://www.ln.edu.hk), Hong Kong, enrolled in the Double PhD Degree Programme jointly with [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn). My supervisors are Prof. QIU Dongxiao Larry (Lingnan) and Prof. YU Zhen (Wuhan).

My research interests are **Innovation**, **International Trade**, and **Trade Policy**, with a focus on China's economy.
