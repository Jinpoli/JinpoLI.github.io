I am a Post-Doctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics at [Lingnan University](https://www.ln.edu.hk), Hong Kong, working with Prof. QIU Dongxiao Larry. I received my Double PhD in Economics jointly from Lingnan University and [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn), supervised by Prof. QIU Dongxiao Larry and Prof. YU Zhen.

My research interests are **Innovation**, **International Trade**, and **Trade Policy**, with a focus on China's economy.
